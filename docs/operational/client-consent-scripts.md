## 1. Standard Plain English Consent Script (In-Person Consultations)

> **To be read aloud by the adviser before starting any recording:**

*"Before we begin, I would like to ask if you are happy for us to record the audio of our conversation today.*

*We use a secure computer tool called Case Ace to help me write up our case notes. This means I can focus completely on listening to you and giving you advice, rather than looking down at my keyboard to type.*

*Here is how we protect your privacy:*
- *Your names, date of birth, address, and National Insurance number are automatically muted and removed right here on this computer before any notes are created.*
- *The recording is completely deleted as soon as our session finishes.*
- *The audio is never shared with the DWP, the council, the Home Office, or anyone else.*

*You do not have to agree to this. If you say no, I will write our notes by hand or type them myself, and your advice, appointment time, and service will be exactly the same.*

*Are you happy for me to turn on the recording?"*

## 3. Telephone Consent Script (Cisco Webex Calls)

> **To be spoken naturally at the start of telephone consultations:**

*"Hello, my name is [Adviser Name] from Citizens Advice Wandsworth.*

*Before we start, I'd like to ask if you agree to me recording the audio of our phone call today using our secure note-writing assistant.*

*The tool mutes all your personal details like your name and postcode so they stay private, and the recording is permanently deleted as soon as we finish our call.*

*You don't have to agree—if you'd rather I didn't record, I will write your notes by hand, and your advice will be exactly the same.*

*Are you happy for me to record the call for note writing?"*

## 5. Adviser Guidance: Handling Client Questions

| Client Question | Approved Adviser Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"Is this shared with the DWP or the Council?"** | *"No, absolutely not. Citizens Advice is completely independent of the government, the DWP, and the council. The recording stays on this computer and is deleted immediately after our session."* |
| **"Can the police or Home Office access this?"** | *"No. The recording is never saved to the cloud or saved to a disk file. Once our note is written into our confidential case file, the audio is destroyed."* |
| **"What happens if I change my mind halfway through?"** | *"Just tell me at any time. I will immediately stop the recording and delete what was captured, and we will continue our session taking notes by hand."* |
| **"Does the computer make decisions about my case?"** | *"No. The computer only helps write down what we discussed. I personally review, edit, and check every single word to ensure all our advice and action steps are 100% correct."* |
