## 1. Executive Authorization Statement

The Board of Trustees and the Senior Leadership Team of **Citizens Advice Wandsworth (CAW)**, having reviewed the complete **Compliance Evidence Pack** (`evidence/`), the **Phase 19 Documentation Set** (`docs/`), and the **16-Point Pilot Readiness Verification Matrix** ([`evidence/pilot_readiness_checklist.md`](../../evidence/pilot_readiness_checklist.md)), hereby **formally authorize the launch of the 6-week Case Ace v2.0 Pilot Deployment**.

This authorization confirms that all technical constraints, security controls, privacy safeguards, and operational procedures are fully operational and verified.

---

## 2. Formal Board & Management Resolutions

1. **Resolution 1 (Data Protection & DPIA v2)**: The Board notes the approval of DPIA Version 2 by the Data Protection Officer and approves the residual risk register, confirming that in-memory JavaScript processing with BitLocker Full Disk Encryption provides appropriate technical and organizational measures under UK GDPR Article 32.
2. **Resolution 2 (Human Professional Accountability)**: The Board confirms that Case Ace is strictly an assistive drafting tool. Qualified advisers retain sole professional responsibility for all case notes recorded in Casebook CRM.
3. **Resolution 3 (Pilot Circuit Breakers)**: The Board formally adopts the Pilot Hard Stop Criteria ([`docs/pilot/pilot-stop-criteria.md`](./pilot-stop-criteria.md)) and vests unilateral, non-discretionary stop authority in the Head of Operations and the Lead Supervising Adviser.
4. **Resolution 4 (National Alignment)**: The Board acknowledges the ongoing reporting cadence established with the Citizens Advice National AI Working Group.
