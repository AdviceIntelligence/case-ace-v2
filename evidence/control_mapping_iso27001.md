## 1. Governance & Boundary Notice

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Declaration of Control Implementation**:
> This document maps the technical, architectural, and operational controls implemented in Case Ace v2.0 against the requirements of **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A**. 
> **No claim of formal accredited third-party certification is made.** This matrix provides empirical, verifiable evidence of compliance for internal audit and information governance oversight.
